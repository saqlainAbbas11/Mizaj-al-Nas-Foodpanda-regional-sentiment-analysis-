How routing works

1

Language detection classifies your review as English, Roman Urdu, or Urdu Script

2

Both models run on your review — producing two independent predictions and confidence scores

3

Language flag determines routing: Roman Urdu goes to the specialist, others to the general model

4

If models disagree or confidence < 0.60, the review would be flagged for LLM review in the full pipeline
