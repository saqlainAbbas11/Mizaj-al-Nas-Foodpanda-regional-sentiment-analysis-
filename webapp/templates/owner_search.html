How the owner dashboard works

Your review data goes through a 10-stage NLP pipeline before reaching this dashboard.

1

Dual-model scoring

Every review is scored by two transformer models — a general multilingual model and a Roman Urdu specialist. Disagreements are flagged, not hidden.

2

7 keyword categories (taste, price, delivery, hygiene, packaging, quantity, service) with multilingual dictionaries catch specific complaints.

3

AI assistant

The chatbot is grounded in your real aggregated data — sentiment mix, aspect breakdowns, and top complaints — so every answer is specific to your restaurant.
